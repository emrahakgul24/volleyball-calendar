How it works

  • iPhone, iPad, Mac: tap Apple Calendar and confirm Subscribe.
  • Google Calendar: tap Google Calendar while signed in, then Add. On Android, add it on calendar.google.com once and it syncs to the phone. Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours to once a day.
  • Anything else: use Copy link and paste it as a calendar subscription (“From URL”).
  • Matches with no announced start time appear as all-day events until the federation publishes the time.

This calendar is free and ad-free. If it helps you follow the matches, you can support it with a coffee.
